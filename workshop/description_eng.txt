[TAROT SATCHEL]

Turn tarot cards into a one-shot out-of-combat resource that drops naturally and can be used.

FEATURES
- After winning a combat, a drop is rolled with a pity system: 15% initially, +10% to the next chance after a miss, -20% after a drop, +12.5% from Elites; Boss fights use the same drop rate as normal combats.
- A dropped tarot appears as a reward on the Combat Rewards screen; click to claim it into the "Tarot Satchel" on the top bar.
- The satchel sits opposite the potion slots, holds 2 slots by default and is always visible like the potion slots.
- Outside combat, click a tarot icon to open "Use / Discard"; it cannot be used during combat.
- After use, the tarot is added to your draw pile at the start of your next combat and can be played normally.
- One-shot: when that combat ends it is removed from the pile whether or not you played it, and it never enters your persistent deck.
- The drop is pre-rolled and written at each save point: loading a save, save & quit and QuickSL reloads never reroll it.

SCOPE
- Works for all characters; unaffected by Ascension level.
- Uses the game's 22 built-in tarot cards; adds no new cards.
- No mod dependencies.

INSTALL
- Subscribe on the Steam Workshop and it is enabled directly; or put the mod folder into the game's mods folder and launch the game through Steam.
- This mod changes gameplay: it uses modded saves; in multiplayer, every player must install it.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Multiplayer: satchel contents are saved locally per player and are not synced.
- Combat encounters triggered by event options (such as the beloved Punch Off event, where two Punch Constructs brawl) do not drop tarots for now.

MOD COMPATIBILITY
- Tested for compatibility with: QuickSL, BaseLib, MintySpire2, Intent Graph, LieRenTVmod.
