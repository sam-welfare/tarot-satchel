[TAROT SATCHEL]

Turn tarot cards into a one-shot resource you can carry out of combat and spend in your next fight.

FEATURES
- Win a combat to roll for a tarot drop with an adaptive pity system: 15% initially, +10% after a miss, -20% after a drop, +12.5% on elites. Bosses use the normal encounter chance.
- A dropped tarot appears as a card-form reward. Claim it to store it in the satchel on the top bar.
- The satchel sits opposite the potion bar, holds 2 tarots by default (capacity is capped at 3) and stays visible for the whole run.
- Outside combat only: click a slot to Use / Discard / Cancel. Slots cannot be used during combat.
- Using a tarot adds it to your draw pile at the start of your next combat, where it can be played normally.
- One-shot: when that combat ends the tarot is removed whether you played it or not, and it never enters your persistent deck.
- The drop is pre-rolled and stored at each official checkpoint, so loading a save, save & quit and QuickSL reloads never reroll: pity and satchel state stay consistent.

SCOPE
- All characters, including modded ones; no ascension changes.
- Uses the game's existing 22-card tarot pool; adds no new cards.
- No dependencies on other mods.

INSTALL
- Subscribe on the Steam Workshop and enable it, or drop the mod folder into the game's mods/ directory and launch from Steam.
- This mod changes gameplay: it uses modded saves, and every player needs it installed for multiplayer.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Multiplayer: satchel contents are tracked locally per player and are not synced.
- Combat encounters started from event options do not roll tarots yet (event combats produce no checkpoint, so the pre-roll cannot be fixed in the save).
- Claiming while the satchel is full is refused with the full-satchel feedback; use or discard a tarot first (no replace-picker popup yet).

MOD COMPATIBILITY
- Tested together with: QuickSL (13 quick save-loads, states stayed consistent), BaseLib, MintySpire2, Intent Graph, LieRenTVmod and Mesugaki_Regent.
- If you find a conflict, leave a comment on the Workshop page with %APPDATA%\SlayTheSpire2\logs\godot.log attached.
