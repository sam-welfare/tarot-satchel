TAROT SATCHEL

Turns the tarot cards that normally only exist inside combat into a collectible resource you can carry into your next fight.

FEATURES
- Combat wins roll for a tarot drop with an adaptive pity system (starts at 15%, +10% on a miss, -20% on a drop, +12.5% on elites).
- A dropped tarot appears as a card-form reward. Click it to store it in your satchel.
- The satchel holds 2 tarots, follows you for the whole run and starts empty each run.
- Outside combat only: click a satchel slot to Use or Discard.
- Using a tarot consumes it and adds it to your draw pile at the start of your next combat.
- One-shot: after that combat ends, the tarot is removed from your deck whether you played it or not.

SCOPE
- All characters. No ascension changes.
- Uses the game's existing 22-card tarot pool; adds no new cards.

INSTALL
Drop the mod folder into the game's mods/ directory and launch from Steam.
This mod changes gameplay: it uses modded saves, and every player needs it installed for multiplayer.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Claiming a reward while the satchel is full is refused (the reward stays; discard a tarot and claim again). No replace-picker popup yet.
- Multiplayer: satchel contents are tracked locally per player and are not synced.
